# Contributing

This repository is a map and two guides. The code lives in the bridge repositories listed in the
[README](README.md).

## The contributions that help most

**A bridge for a platform that has none.** If you build a command-line client that lets an agent
drive a participation platform, open a pull request adding it to the table in the README. It
does not have to come from us, be written in Python, or match our command names. It should be
open source, install as a normal package, speak to a platform's own API, and answer in a
machine-readable shape.

**Taking a bridge into its platform's repository.** The bridges here are offered to their
communities. If you maintain Pol.is, Voxit, CONSUL DEMOCRACY or Decidim and want the bridge in
your tree, the clients are Apache-2.0 precisely so you can vendor them without a licence
conflict, under your own licence and governance. Tell us and we will help with the port and keep
pointing here.

**Corrections to the guides.** The two documents in `docs/` are field notes from four bridges,
not a specification. If something in them is wrong, or true only for one platform, say so.

## Working on a bridge itself

Issues and pull requests belong in that bridge's own repository. Each one carries its own
licence, tests and release process; see its README.

## Licensing your contribution

Contributions to this repository are accepted under Apache-2.0, the licence of the repository.
You keep your copyright. There is no contributor licence agreement and no assignment.

## Conduct

Be decent. This is civic-tech infrastructure built by people who mostly know each other; behave
the way you would at a conference.
